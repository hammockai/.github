# 🏴‍️ Hammock AI — Manifesto

Websites, automations and AI crews for solo dreamers and small businesses.
Self-taught, from Chile, documented in the open.

## Values

- **The Balanced Sea** — We operate by Nash Equilibrium. Mutual benefit > individual advantage. We reject zero-sum thinking and extractive business models.
- **The Booger Rule** — Radical honesty. We point out the problems immediately, but we never do so without handing you the tissue (the solution). No sugarcoating, no hidden risks.
- **Accessibility First** — Technology serves those who start. Our outputs are non-technical friendly, practical over theoretical, and completely free of gatekeeping.
- **Open-Source Ethos** — Knowledge should be free, accessible, and improvable. We share our maps, our prompts, and our workflows so others can remix and build upon them.
- **Pirate Ethics & Sovereign Ship** — Zero telemetry. Zero trackers. Total control. The Captain holds the keys. We do not build dependencies without exit ramps.
